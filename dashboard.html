Layers
L1 Transcribe through L4 Stutter
▶
L1 Transcribe — Whisper only. Raw speech-to-text, no AI cleanup. Fastest.
L2 Reconstruct — GPT rewrites using your tone. Strips fillers, fixes grammar. ~1-2s slower.
L3 Profile — Same as L2 + your personal vocabulary and corrections map.
L4 Stutter — Full clinical mode. Disfluency tracking, exposure scoring, severity-scaled rewrites.
You say: "So uh basically the the thing is um working"
L1: "So uh basically the the thing is um working"
L2: "The thing is working."

▶

Professional — Business-ready. Clean, precise language.

Mode
Pipeline steps: RAW, FAST, SAFE
▶
RAW paste as-is — GPT runs for analytics but pastes Whisper's original text.
FAST skip validation — GPT rewrites, trust it, paste. Best speed/quality.
SAFE + Falcon check — GPT rewrites, Falcon verifies meaning preserved. Slowest, safest.

All three produce identical output on L1. Collapses when L1 is selected.

DAF
Delayed Auditory Feedback for fluency
▶
Plays your voice back through headphones with a slight delay. The delay tricks the brain into slowing down, improving fluency. Independent of all other settings.

Toggle: ON/OFF   Dial: Delay in milliseconds (default 100ms). More = stronger effect.

Whisper Diagnostics
Multi-temp, blocks, confidence
▶
Multi-Temp — Runs Whisper at multiple temperatures. Agreement = confident. Disagreement = flagged. 3x slower.
Blocks — Speech blocks detected (Whisper hallucinated over silence).
No Speech slider — ◀ Sensitive (catches more) vs. Tolerant ▶ (fewer false alarms). Default: 15%.
Avg LogProb — Whisper confidence. Above -0.4 = good. Below -0.7 = guessing.

How It Works
What happens when you press Record
▶
1. You speak
Your voice is captured through the microphone. The audio is cleaned up (background noise removed, volume leveled) before anything else touches it.

2. Whisper listens
OpenAI's Whisper converts your speech to text. It also reports how confident it is about each word. Quiet moments (blocks, pauses) are flagged rather than guessed at.
If you use Script Prep, your intended text helps Whisper guess better — like giving it the answer key.
3. Quick cleanup
Before any AI gets involved, obvious disfluencies are stripped automatically (zero cost, instant):
• Repeated syllables: "p-p-pop" → "pop"
• Repeated words: "I I I want" → "I want"
• Fillers: "um", "uh", "like", "you know"
• False starts: "going to— I went" → "I went"
• Prolongations: "mmmmaybe" → "maybe"
Natural phrases like "bye bye" and "had had" are kept — they're real grammar, not disfluencies.
4. What happens next depends on your Layer:

Layer 1 — Done. The cleaned text is pasted. No AI, no internet after Whisper, no cost beyond transcription.

Layer 2 — GPT rewrites your text using your chosen tone (casual, professional, etc). It knows the text came from a microphone, not a keyboard, so it's forgiving about messiness. No personal data is used.

Layer 3 — Same as Layer 2, plus GPT knows your preferred words and corrections. If it's learned that you always say "gonna" but mean "going to" in professional mode, it fixes that automatically.

Layer 4 — The full engine. GPT switches to a stronger model and gets a detailed briefing:
• Which sounds are hardest for you (your personal phoneme map)
• Words you tend to avoid and swap for easier ones
• Where Whisper was uncertain or detected a block
• Non-verbal events (coughs, laughter, pauses) so it doesn't hallucinate words over them
• Your pitch, energy, and speaking rate patterns

5. Safety check
In SAFE mode, a second AI (Falcon) reads both your original words and the rewrite, and asks: "Did the meaning change?" If yes, the rewrite is rejected and your original text is used instead. Numbers, dollar amounts, and percentages are double-checked separately.

6. Paste
The final text goes to your clipboard and is pasted into whatever app you're using.

Command Mode (F8)
Transform highlighted text with voice
▶
Instead of dictating new text, transform text you already have. Works in any app.

How to use:
1. Select/highlight text anywhere (email, doc, Slack, etc.)
2. Hold F8 and speak a command
3. Release F8 — your selection is replaced with the transformed text

Example commands: "make this formal", "fix the grammar", "translate to Spanish", "summarize this", "make it shorter", "rewrite this as bullet points"
Selected: "hey can u send me that report when u get a chance"
F8 + say: "make this professional"
Replaced with: "Could you please send me that report at your earliest convenience?"
Your clipboard is preserved. Works at any layer/tone. Uses GPT-4o.

Quiet Mode
For whispered or soft speech
▶
Toggle in the Voice section of the sidebar. When ON:
• High-pass filter tightens from 70Hz to 100Hz (kills more room noise since whispers have no low-frequency content)
• Audio gain doubles (-6dB vs -12dB target) to boost soft speech

Use it when dictating in shared spaces where you don't want to be heard (library, open office, late at night). Stuttering can worsen under social pressure — this lets you dictate without drawing attention.

Privacy & Your Data
What we keep, how to export/delete
▶
Your voice audio is never stored. It's sent to Whisper for transcription, then discarded.

Your transcripts stay on YOUR computer (in ~/.lavrentiy/). They never leave your device.

If you sign in with Google: Only pattern metadata syncs to the cloud (trigger words, filler words, vocabulary, corrections, onset weights). Never raw text or audio.

Export / Delete: Sign in, go to The File tab, click EXPORT to download all your data as JSON, or DELETE CLOUD DATA to wipe your Firestore record (local data stays).

Retention: Cloud data auto-deletes after 24 months of inactivity.

Loops & Blocks
Whisper quality diagnostics
▶
Loops (learn bar) — Count of Whisper hallucination loops this session. When Whisper gets stuck repeating ("bad bad bad..." / "Thank you Thank you..."), its compression ratio spikes. We detect + strip these segments automatically.

Block suspects (console, brown text) — Segments where Whisper nearly classified the audio as silence but output text anyway. For stutterers, that "silence" is often a block. We strip short phrases (≤3 words) Whisper hallucinated into blocks.

Known hallucinations auto-stripped: "Transcribed by otter.ai", "Thank you for watching", "Subscribe to my channel", "Subtitles by Amara.org" — these leak in from Whisper's YouTube training data.
